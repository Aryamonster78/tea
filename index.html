About Gajadhar & Son

Gajadhar & Son Company is a Rajasthan-based tea business dedicated to bringing genuine taste and freshness to every cup. We focus on careful selection, honest sourcing and consistent quality, so every pack of tea that leaves our hands feels worth the trust placed in it.

From strong Assam blends to light green tea, our goal stays simple — a cup that tastes right, every single time.
